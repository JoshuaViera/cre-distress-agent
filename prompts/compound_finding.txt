COMPOUND FINDING TASK

You have today's structured tool outputs (above) and the snapshot_diff temporal vectors from the prior snapshot. Your job is to surface CROSS-SIGNAL compound findings — situations where two or more signals jointly imply something that neither signal implies on its own.

Examples of valid compound findings:
  - "10Y Treasury rose +30 bps AND ACRIS comp velocity in the submarket dropped 40% week-over-week" → cap rates likely widening; the deal's exit cap assumption is at heightened risk.
  - "HPD class-C violations jumped from 1 to 4 AND in-place rent is 9% below market" → tenant retention risk is now compounding with re-leasing risk; underwritten rollover assumptions may understate downtime.
  - "Observed market rent dropped 8% AND treasury rose 25 bps" → both NOI and exit valuation pressured; NOI/IRR delta in the underwriting tool understates the full picture because it only models one observed input at a time.

Constraints (HARD):
  1. Each finding MUST cite at least TWO specific named signals from the tool outputs above (e.g., "macro_signals.treasury_10y", "violations.severity_breakdown.C", "market_signals.sale_count").
  2. Each finding MUST cite the snapshot_diff field(s) that anchor it temporally (e.g., "snapshot_diff: tool_outputs.macro_signals.treasury_10y.bps_change yesterday=5 today=30") OR explicitly note that no prior snapshot exists yet.
  3. Each finding must include one short analyst-actionable recommendation.
  4. If you cannot construct a finding meeting all three constraints, output exactly:
       ## Compound Findings

       No compound findings this run.

  5. Do not restate single-signal findings already covered in the synthesis briefing.
  6. Do not invent numbers or signals not present in the tool outputs.

Output format (markdown, appended to the synthesis briefing):

## Compound Findings

### <Short title of finding 1>
- **Signals:** <signal A>, <signal B>[, <signal C>...]
- **Diff anchor:** <snapshot_diff field path>: yesterday=<value> today=<value> (or "no prior snapshot")
- **Implication:** <one sentence explaining what the combination means that neither signal means alone>
- **Recommendation:** <one short analyst action>

### <Short title of finding 2>
... (repeat as needed) ...

Output ONLY the markdown section. No commentary, no preamble.
