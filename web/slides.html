The Problem

Five tabs. One tired analyst. Signals caught too late.

A junior CRE analyst spends 2-3 hours daily refreshing CoStar, FRED, and city portals — checking if pipeline deals still pencil. Integration happens in their head. Material changes get caught late.

Cost of a Miss

~$10M

A $12/SF rent drift on 85K SF is ~$470K/yr in NOI. At a 5.5% cap, that's nearly $10M in value — often caught after LOI.

Daily Manual Scans

3 hrs/day

HPD violations, ACRIS comps, FRED rates — all by hand, across multiple tabs, every morning before standup.

No Signal Integration

0

No system connects "rates moved" to "your IRR dropped 3 points." That math lives in the analyst's head.
